# Joby Aviation (NYSE: JOBY): SELL / Underweight

**Price:** $5.16 ([Yahoo Finance, Nov 4, 2026 intraday](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/JOBY/)) · **Probability-weighted value:** $4.19 (−19%) · **Range:** $0.00 (bear) / $1.00 (base) / $14.76 (bull) · **Street mean target:** $10.01 from 9 analysts ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/JOBY/analysis/))

**One-liner:** Joby has the most cash of the listed eVTOL companies and is well along the certification path, but at $5 the stock already prices in a fleet roughly 3x my base case, while rising cash burn means more dilution before profits arrive.

## Thesis

1. **The price already assumes the bull case is likely.** Holding my base-case economics fixed ($3.0M revenue per aircraft, 30% EBITDA margin at scale, 15% discount rate), today's price requires **~2,640 aircraft in service by 2035 vs. 800 in my base case** (reverse DCF on the [Valuation page](/valuation)). My scenarios give $0 / $1.00 / $14.76. Weighted 25/50/25, that is **$4.19**, 19% below the market. Most of that value comes from the bull case (25% × $14.76 ≈ $3.69).
2. **Funding needs mean more dilution before profit.** Free-cash-flow burn was **$201.8M in Q2 2026** and **$743M over the last four quarters**, against **$2.26B of cash and short-term investments** at June 30, 2026 ([10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1819848/000181984826000435/joby-20260630.htm)). That is ~11 quarters of runway, or **~9 quarters pro forma** for the ~$450M cash portion of the pending Resonant Sciences acquisition ([8-K, Aug 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1819848/000162828026055505/joby-20260808.htm)). Joby has set up a **$750M at-the-market stock program** ([424B5](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1819848/000162828026055507/atm424b5.htm)), and its share count is already **up 64% since Aug 2021** (603.9M to 989.1M, per 10-Q/10-K cover pages). My base case still has a **~$1.7B funding gap** before free cash flow turns positive.
3. **Execution is still binary, and the lead is not guaranteed.** Joby says it is "approximately 75% complete" with stage 4 of 5 of FAA type certification and is "targeting carrying our first passengers in 2026" ([10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1819848/000181984826000435/joby-20260630.htm)). Early flights in Florida under the FAA's eVTOL pilot program are real progress ([Yahoo Finance, Nov 2026](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/joby-aviation-joby-begins-piloted-131533650.html)). But scaled US revenue needs type certification and a production ramp. Competitors are close: BETA reached an FAA milestone first ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/beta-technologies-beat-archer-joby-133500120.html)), and Archer's ITC patent case targets Joby's imported components (10-Q legal proceedings).

## Valuation in one paragraph

Scenario DCF: project fleet × revenue per aircraft, blend from today's burn to an at-scale margin, subtract aircraft capex and tax, and discount at 13–18% to a terminal value at 8–15x EBITDA. Then add net cash (cash $2.26B, debt $0.70B, less ~$0.40B burn before 2027) and divide by shares after 15–50% future dilution. Interactive model and sources are on the [Valuation page](/valuation). Comps: Joby's EV (~$3.6B) is the second highest among listed eVTOL peers, behind BETA (see the comps table).

## Key risks to my call (why I could be wrong)

- **Certification arrives on time and the ramp is fast.** The price is most sensitive to fleet size, margins, launch year and ramp speed (tornado chart). Moving launch to 2026, or raising fleet at scale to 1,200, each lifts the base value from $1.00 to about $1.66 / $1.91 on its own.
- **The bull case is more likely than 25%.** Holding the bear case at 25%, a bull probability above ~26% moves the call to HOLD, and above ~38% to BUY. That is a thin margin, so this is a valuation call, not a "Joby fails" call.
- **Strategic support.** Toyota, Delta, Uber and the US Department of Defense are partners or investors ([10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1819848/000181984826000160/joby-20251231.htm)). Air Force autonomy contracts were expanded to nearly $37M on Nov 4, 2026 ([Business Wire via Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/joby-expands-autonomous-flight-contracts-130000124.html)). Non-dilutive funding like this reduces the dilution risk.

## What would change my mind

- FAA type certification issued (or the final for-credit testing stage completed) with a firm US launch date.
- Disclosed unit economics from Dubai or Florida operations showing ≥$3M revenue per aircraft per year.
- Evidence that cost per aircraft is falling toward ~$1.5M (Toyota production partnership), or funding that does not need stock sales.
- Price alone: at my current assumptions, a share price below ~$4.93 would make this a HOLD (within ±15% of $4.19), and below ~$3.64 a BUY.
